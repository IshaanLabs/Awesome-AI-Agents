## Open Questions / Risks / Blockers
### Open Questions

- What are the exact technical details and implementation strategies for integrating agentic AI features within the mobile banking app?
- How will the state management mechanism work in practice, especially when dealing with sensitive user data like bank statements?
- What specific human-in-the-loop processes need to be established, and how will they impact the overall user experience?

### Risks

#### ### Data Security and Privacy
- **Risk Name:** Inadequate Data Encryption and Access Controls  
  **Explanation:** Ensuring that local storage of sensitive information such as bank statements is secure and properly encrypted is crucial. If not managed correctly, there could be significant security breaches leading to data theft or misuse.
  - **Potential Impact:** A data breach could result in financial loss for users, damage the company’s reputation, and lead to legal consequences.

#### ### Connectivity Issues
- **Risk Name:** Intermittent Internet Connection Leading to Data Inconsistencies  
  **Explanation:** The app needs to handle scenarios where internet connectivity is inconsistent or lost. If not properly managed, this could result in outdated data being displayed to users.
  - **Potential Impact:** Users might make decisions based on incorrect information, leading to potential financial errors and customer dissatisfaction.

#### ### Integration Complexity
- **Risk Name:** Incompatibility Between Agentic AI Features and Existing Systems  
  **Explanation:** Integrating agentic AI features like case registration into the mobile banking app could introduce compatibility issues with existing systems. This might require significant rework or additional development time.
  - **Potential Impact:** Delays in project timelines and increased costs due to unforeseen integration challenges.

#### ### Human-in-the-Loop Implementation
- **Risk Name:** Ineffective Human-in-the-Loop Processes  
  **Explanation:** The human-in-the-loop feature, while intended to enhance decision-making, could be poorly implemented if not properly designed. This might lead to delays in processing or incorrect decisions being made.
  - **Potential Impact:** Reduced efficiency and accuracy of the app’s operations, potentially leading to customer complaints and decreased user trust.
