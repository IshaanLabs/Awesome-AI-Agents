## Key Technical / Business Discussions

### 1. App Development Overview
- **Discussion:** The meeting began with Oswald Lopes outlining the need to develop two mobile apps: Mobile Banking and AML.
- **Requirements Discussed:**
  - Both apps will include chat-based interactions.
  - AML app will incorporate agentic AI for case registration.

### 2. Session Management and Local Storage
- **Discussion:** The team discussed session management, particularly the handling of local storage for the last five statements.
- **Details:**
  - When connected to the internet, the mobile app should refresh the last five statements stored locally.
  - The state is maintained in a local database but synchronized with backend data.

### 3. Demonstration Flows
- **Discussion:** Oswald Lopes emphasized the need for clear demonstration flows that combine chat-based interactions and form submissions.
- **Examples:**
  - A journey involving chat, followed by a form submission, then a complete chat-based interaction was discussed.

---

## Decisions Made
1. Develop two mobile apps: Mobile Banking and AML.
2. Implement session management with local storage for the last five statements.
3. Ensure synchronization of local data with backend systems when connected to the internet.
4. Create clear demonstration flows combining chat, form submissions, and complete chat-based interactions.

### Risks
1. **Risk of Inadequate Synchronization:**
   - Risk Description: There is a risk that local storage may not accurately reflect the latest data from the backend, leading to discrepancies.
2. **Risk of Complex Demonstration Flows:**
   - Risk Description: The complexity of integrating different interaction types (chat, form submissions) could lead to delays in development and testing.
3. **Risk of Insufficient Human-in-the-Loop Integration:**
   - Risk Description: Ensuring that the human-in-the-loop process is properly integrated into the AML app may require additional resources and time.

## Technical Requirements & Assumptions

### Functional Requirements
1. Develop a Mobile Banking app with chat-based interactions.
2. Develop an AML app incorporating agentic AI for case registration.
3. Implement session management with local storage for the last five statements.
4. Ensure synchronization of local data with backend systems when connected to the internet.

### Non-functional Requirements
1. Performance: The apps should handle high volumes of user interactions efficiently.
2. Security: Data stored locally and in the backend must be secure.
3. Usability: The apps should provide a seamless user experience, including clear navigation and intuitive interfaces.

### Assumptions
1. Backend systems will support real-time data synchronization.
2. Human-in-the-loop processes are well-defined and can be integrated into the AML app.
3. Development teams have access to necessary tools and resources for testing and deployment.
